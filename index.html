Rainfall outlook (this month)

Per-city posterior over total monthly rainfall — Bayesian gamma fit using observed-so-far + ensemble QPF for the forecast window + climatology for the tail. Mean / median / mode + σ + 68% / 95% CIs come from the gamma posterior directly (Wilson-Hilferty quantiles for the CIs). Mode of a gamma with shape ≥ 1 is at (k−1)θ; gamma is right-skewed so median < mean and mode < median.

Kalshi prediction-market edges

Compares our model's per-bucket probability to current Kalshi daily-high markets for the 13 covered cities. Edge is per-$1 staked, net of Kalshi fees (≈ 0.07 × (1−price) per $1 contract). Educational only — not betting advice. Model RMSE ~1.7°F; small edges are noise.

Sorted by Kelly fraction — optimal stake as % of bankroll given probability + price. Bet half the Kelly value to protect against model error.

The Decision column reflects how the live Jackson trader weighed each edge on its most recent cycle (every 5 min, temp) / (every 30 min, rain). purchased means a real Kalshi buy fired. skipped means a downstream safety gate rejected it (tile-conflict, bucket-margin, tail-posterior, Kelly-LCB shrink, city-pause, cooldown, etc.). below floor means it never reached the trader's qualifying set (edge / half-Kelly / price / volume floor). pending means it qualified but the next trader cycle hasn't run yet (or the candidate appeared after the last cycle).
